Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 March 2026

WildDeck ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal information when you use the WildDeck mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

When you use WildDeck, we may collect the following information: • Account Information: Your name, username, email address, and profile picture you provide during registration. • Wildlife Data: Photos you upload for identification, species identified, discovery locations (if location services are enabled), and your card collection data. • Usage Data: How you interact with the app, including battles played, quests completed, and features used. • Device Information: Device type, operating system, and app version for troubleshooting and performance improvement. • Purchase Data: Transaction records for in-app purchases including physical card orders and coin purchases. We do not store full payment card details.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to: • Provide and improve the WildDeck experience, including wildlife identification, card collection, and battle features. • Process orders for physical cards and deliver them to your specified address. • Personalise your experience, including discovery recommendations and quest suggestions. • Maintain leaderboards, friend connections, and social features. • Send important service updates and notifications (if enabled). • Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, or violations of our terms.

3. Wildlife Photo Processing

Photos you submit for wildlife identification are processed using AI technology. These images are: • Used solely for the purpose of identifying the species in the photo. • Not shared with third parties for advertising purposes. • Processed securely and not stored indefinitely after identification is complete. • Never used to identify or track individuals — only wildlife species.

4. Location Data

If you enable location services: • Your location is used to record where wildlife discoveries are made and display them on your sightings map. • Location data is stored with your discovery records to enhance your collection. • You can disable location services at any time in your device or app settings. • We do not continuously track your location — it is only accessed when you actively make a discovery.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share limited information with: • Service Providers: Trusted partners who help us operate the app (e.g., cloud hosting, payment processing, card printing and delivery). • Other Players: Your public profile (username, avatar, level, and collection stats) is visible to friends and battle opponents. • Legal Requirements: If required by law or to protect the safety of our users.

6. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data, including encryption of data in transit and at rest, secure authentication, and regular security reviews.

7. Children's Privacy

WildDeck is designed to be family-friendly. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us at info@wild-deck.com.

8. Your Rights

Under applicable data protection laws (including UK GDPR), you have the right to: • Access the personal data we hold about you. • Request correction of inaccurate data. • Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten"). • Object to or restrict processing of your data. • Request data portability. • Withdraw consent at any time. To exercise these rights, contact us at info@wild-deck.com.

9. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you with our services. If you delete your account, we will remove your personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the app or via email. Continued use of WildDeck after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us: Email: info@wild-deck.com We aim to respond to all enquiries within 48 hours.
